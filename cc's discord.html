03rules

  1. be nice. people come here to chill. don't make the room worse.
  2. no slurs. any of them. joke or not, lyric or not. say one and you're banned, no warning.
  3. no harassing people. no ganging up, no "it was just banter" after someone says stop. ban.
  4. be gay, idk. be whoever you are. queer, straight, questioning, none of it — nobody's asked to explain themselves.
  5. nothing illegal, nothing under 18. no nsfw in public channels, no adult stuff with minors ever, no piracy, no doxxing. ban and reported.
  6. right channel for the right thing. memes in memes, clips in clips, jailbreaks in jailbreaks. if a mod moves your post they're just tidying.
  7. no spam. one link in the promo channel, not in every chat. dm-advertising gets you muted.
  8. no bringing in outside drama. beef from another server or stream stays out.
  9. mods get the last word. if a call feels wrong, dm a mod politely instead of arguing in general.
  10. have fun, actually. post something, join the call, start a lobby. quiet server is everyone's fault.

2, 3 and 5 are instant bans — no warnings, no "didn't mean it". everything else: warning, then mute, then out.
